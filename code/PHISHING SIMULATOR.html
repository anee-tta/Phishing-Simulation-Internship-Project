Phishing Simulation

Practice spotting phishing emails in a safe environment. You’ll see 5 realistic examples and decide whether each one is phishing or legitimate.

How it works:
• Read each email carefully.
• Choose Phishing or Not phishing.
• At the end, you’ll see your score, your estimated risk level, and the key red flags you may have missed.

This exercise is for training and awareness only. No real credentials are collected. Try to answer based on how you would behave in real life.

When you’re ready, click “Start simulation” to go to Example 1.
